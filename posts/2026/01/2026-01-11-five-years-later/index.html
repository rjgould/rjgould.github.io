Five years later...

Who's got time to blog anymore?

   |  1   |  167   |  Rob Gould

It’s funny how many things seem important in life at the time, but in the long run turn out to not really matter at all. The last time I edited this blog was back in 2021, almost exactly five years ago. It doesn’t seem like that long ago but things in life were very different then. In 2021 I only had one child, both my parents were alive, and I was not as far along in my career as I am now.

At the time, fixing this blog was clearly very important to me. Back then stopping this domain from just sitting here doing nothing seemed like a mountain that had to be climbed. So I climbed it, and when it was done, it was simply forgotten. No longer as pressing a priority in life as it seemed, just something that had been achieved and was now OK to move on from, to start working on something else.

I wonder what the next five years might bring?
